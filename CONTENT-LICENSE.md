## Courses you build with the skill

Those are **your** works, not this repository's. The MIT licence does not give you rights in a certifying body's content
or trademarks, and it does not clear anyone else's material. Before sharing a course, follow
[`skills/cert-course-builder/references/content-rights.md`](skills/cert-course-builder/references/content-rights.md).
In short: original wording only, no real exam items, link to (do not copy) the official blueprint, name the certification
nominatively with a non-affiliation notice, and check any licence a body or standard requires (ITIL, for example).

## Recorded narration (if you add it)

If you generate natural-voice audio for a course:

1. Use a provider and plan whose current terms allow your intended use (some free plans forbid commercial use or require
   attribution); record the terms and the date you checked them in the course's `RIGHTS.md`.
2. Tell listeners the voices are AI-generated (some providers require this).
3. Never clone a real person's voice without their written consent.
4. Keep audio out of this repository unless its terms permit redistribution.
