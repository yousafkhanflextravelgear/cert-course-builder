# Content rights

The full guidance lives with the skill so that it travels with it:
**[`skills/cert-course-builder/references/content-rights.md`](../skills/cert-course-builder/references/content-rights.md)**
(rules, per-body findings with sources, decision table, required notices, three sharing gates) and the fill-in
**[rights record](../skills/cert-course-builder/references/rights-record-template.md)**.

The short version:

- A course may **describe and teach** what an exam covers; it may not **reproduce** the body's protected material or any
  real exam item.
- Write everything in your own words from public standards and general knowledge. Link to the official blueprint; do not
  copy it.
- Name the certification factually, add a non-affiliation notice, and use no logos.
- Some bodies require a **licence** for third-party courses (ITIL is one). Without it, keep the course private.
- Check the terms of any recorded-voice provider, disclose AI voices, and self-host fonts.
- Run `scan_publish_safety.py` and complete `RIGHTS.md` before sharing.

This is a practical checklist, not legal advice.
